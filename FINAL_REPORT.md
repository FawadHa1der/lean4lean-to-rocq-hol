## 1. Executive Summary

This repository contains two independent, machine-checked formalizations of the **Lean 4 kernel (`lean4lean`)** in **Rocq 9.2.0** and **HOL Light 3.1.0**:

1. **Executable Kernel Specification & Extraction (`Syntax.v`, `Universes.v`, `Checker.v`, `LookupCore.v`, `Declarations.v`, `Inductive.v`, `NestedTransform.v`, `NestedAdmission.v`, `Quotient.v`, `Primitives.v` / `hol/kernel_v3_complete/hol/`)**:

### 3.1 Semantic De Bruijn Algebra & Universe Polymorphism (`rocq/SemanticModel.v` & `declarative_typing961.ml` / `typing_lemmas966.ml`)

In [`rocq/SemanticModel.v`](rocq/SemanticModel.v) (874 lines) and [`hol/kernel_v3_complete/hol/declarative_typing961.ml`](hol/kernel_v3_complete/hol/declarative_typing961.ml) + [`typing_lemmas966.ml`](hol/kernel_v3_complete/hol/typing_lemmas966.ml), we formalized:
- **Semantic Universe Evaluation & Equivalence**:
  - `vlevel_eval (ls : list nat) (u : VLevel) : nat` (`l4_vlevel_eval961`) with `imax` semantics (`0` when the codomain evaluates to `0`, `Nat.max` otherwise).
  - Semantic ordering `vlevel_le` and equivalence `vlevel_equiv` (`l4_vlevel_equiv961`), proved reflexive, symmetric, transitive, congruent under `succ`, `max`, `imax`, and preserved under universe substitution (`vlevel_inst` / `l4_vlevel_inst961`).
  - Bridge theorems `vlevel_of_level_wf`, `vlevel_of_level_eval`, `check_level_sound_vlevel`, and `level_equiv_sound_vlevel` connecting executable universe checking to semantic well-formedness and equivalence.
- **De Bruijn Lifting & Instantiation Commutation Suite**:
  - `vliftN_zero`: $\uparrow_k^0 e = e$
  - `vliftN_vliftN` (`l4_vliftN_vliftN966`): $\uparrow_{k'}^{n} (\uparrow_k^m e) = \uparrow_k^{m+n} e$ for $k \le k' \le m + k$
  - `vliftN_comm` (`l4_vliftN_comm966`): $\uparrow_{k'}^{n} (\uparrow_k^m e) = \uparrow_{n+k}^m (\uparrow_{k'}^n e)$ for $k' \le k$
  - `vliftN_vinst_hi` (`l4_vliftN_vinst_hi966`): $\uparrow_k^n (e[v/k']) = (\uparrow_k^n e)[v / (n + k')]$ for $k \le k'$
  - `vliftN_vinst_lo` (`l4_vliftN_vinst_lo966`): $\uparrow_{k'}^n (e[v/k]) = (\uparrow_{S k'}^n e)[\uparrow_{k'-k}^n v / k]$ for $k \le k'$
  - `vinst_vinst_hi` and `vinst0_vinst_hi` (`l4_vinst_vinst_hi966`, `l4_vinst0_vinst_hi966`): $(e_1[v_1 / 0])[v_2 / k] = (e_1[\uparrow_0^1 v_2 / S k])[v_1[v_2 / k] / 0]$
  - Universe instantiation commutation: `vinstL_params`, `vinstL_vliftN`, `vinstL_vinst`, `vinstL_vinstL` (`l4_vinstL_params966`, `l4_vinstL_vliftN966`, `l4_vinstL_vinst966`, `l4_vinstL_vinstL966`).
