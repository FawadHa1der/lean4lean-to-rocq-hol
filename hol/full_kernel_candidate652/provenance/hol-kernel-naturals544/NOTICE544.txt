Full447 syntax/full491 results/full493 primitive tags and533 names/readiness
are reused without replacing their types or copying a reduced representation.
The four requested operations are actual HOL object-language definitions.
Two supporting natural-number definitions implement a low-bit fold with
input-derived width MAX(bitsize a)(bitsize b),not fixed-width words or caller
resource fuel. This helper is used only for AND/OR/XOR,not the PNatBitwise tag.
The latter retains Accept NONE,as do the three other non-binary cases.

Installed Library/bitsize.ml,Library/binary.ml,Library/prime.ml and underlying
arithmetic/int definitions were inspected. The implementation reuses bitsize,
natural arithmetic and gcd(a,b). The binary-set theory is an independent
mathematical specification candidate,not a runtime delegate or loaded module.
Existing hol/nat_evaluation.ml proves a conditional recurrence theorem over
legacy expressions,not a full447 primitive evaluator. The old shift_left_budget
module is host allocation preflight; neither is imported or used as a verdict.

Mathematical totality is not executable evaluation validation. bitsize is
defined using minimal; natural gcd maps through integer gcd theory. Their
proof-producing conversions are installed,but have not been integrated or
executed in this task. The fold additionally needs checked width computation
and arithmetic evaluation. The four supplementary proposed numeric regressions
refer to these conversions but are ALL UNCOMPILED,not conversion-run evidence.

The handwritten model uses finite integer arithmetic,Euclidean gcd and a
division-based bit-length calculation. Those are not the executable HOL
definitions and do not prove an encoding,extraction or implementation relation.
No new axioms,admitted proofs,unchecked casts or evaluator/verdict oracle are
introduced. Universal numeric/bitwise/Rocq correspondence remains unproved.
