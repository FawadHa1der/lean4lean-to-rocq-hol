This port preserves all fields present in syntax447. The input representation
already erases metadata payloads and represents strings as num lists. This
package performs no additional erasure and does not use a restricted bridge.
Metadata wrappers, structured names, term and level metavariables, binderInfo,
nonDep, projection fields and literal contents are retained. It does not make
syntax447 a lossless Lean serialization or establish full compatibility.
